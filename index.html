Application Process

Perfect Walls in 4 Simple Steps

01
Surface Prep
Clean and dampen the wall. Remove loose paint or dust for optimal adhesion.
02
Mix Plaster
Add Royal White Homes HDMR powder to clean water. Mix to a smooth, lump-free consistency.
03
Apply & Level
Apply with a steel trowel in 2 coats. Level and compress for a seamless finish.
04
Final Polish
Once dried, lightly sand and proceed directly to painting. No putty required.
